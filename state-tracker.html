⬜ What does "no law" mean for you?

The majority of US states have not yet passed legislation specifically addressing plug-in solar. Without a state law, the relevant rules are your utility's own interconnection requirements — and these vary significantly by utility. Some utilities have explicit policies for small generating facilities; others have nothing on the books at all. We strongly recommend contacting your utility or reviewing their interconnection tariff before installing any system. This site provides general information only and is not a substitute for reviewing the rules that apply to your specific address. When in doubt, consult a licensed electrician or attorney familiar with your state's regulations.
